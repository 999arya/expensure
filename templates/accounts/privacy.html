Privacy, Consent & Security

How Expensure protects your financial information and what consent you provide.

1. Estimated Balances (No Live Bank Connection)

Expensure does not connect directly to any banking API, does not have banking login credentials, and cannot initiate any money transfers. All balances shown on this platform are estimates calculated from user-confirmed balance checkpoints and manually recorded or SMS-captured movements.

2. SMS Capture & Privacy Guarantees (Decision 006)

When you set up message ingestion via Apple Shortcuts or manual paste:

  • No raw message bodies are stored: The server parses the message deterministically, extracts the amount, direction, account suffix and reference, and discards the raw SMS body immediately.
  • Automatic OTP rejection: Verification codes and OTPs are detected and rejected. They are never recorded or processed.
  • Device tokens: Ingestion endpoints require pre-authorized device tokens whose secret values are stored hashed (SHA-256) and can be revoked at any time.

3. AI Processing Policy

When you request AI insights or affordability questions:

  • Calculations are performed in Python first. The AI receives only minimal numerical summaries without your name, email, or full account numbers.
  • AI can recommend a monthly savings target from calculated history. Applying it requires your confirmation and records the old and new target. AI cannot move funds.
