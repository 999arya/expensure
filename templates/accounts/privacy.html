Privacy, Consent & Security

How Expensure protects your financial information and what consent you provide.

1. Estimated Balances (No Live Bank Connection)

Expensure does not connect directly to any banking API, does not have banking login credentials, and cannot initiate any money transfers. All balances shown on this platform are estimates calculated from user-confirmed balance checkpoints and movements you recorded by hand or imported from a CSV statement.

2. Statement Import & Privacy Guarantees (Decision 006)

When you import a Kotak or Bank of Baroda CSV statement:

  • No statement file is stored: The server reads the CSV in memory, extracts the date, description, amount, direction and reference, and discards the file immediately.
  • Temporary preview rows: Normalized rows exist only until you confirm or cancel the import. After that, only counts and batch metadata are kept so you can review or undo the import.
  • Only the last four digits: The app stores the bank name and last four account digits, never the full account number or net-banking password.

3. AI Processing Policy

When you request AI insights or affordability questions:

  • Calculations are performed in Python first. The AI receives only minimal numerical summaries without your name, email, or full account numbers.
  • AI can recommend a monthly savings target from calculated history. Applying it requires your confirmation and records the old and new target. AI cannot move funds.
