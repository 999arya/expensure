Guided iPhone Setup

APPLE SHORTCUTS INGESTION
  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Automation tab at the bottom, then tap + in the top-right.
  3. Choose Message as the trigger.
  4. Under Message Contains, enter: Kotak, debited, credited, Baroda.
  5. Select Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run.
  6. Tap Next and choose New Blank Automation.
  7. Add the action: Get Contents of URL:
    • URL: {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}{% url 'ingestion:api_ingest' %}
    • Method: POST
    • Headers:
      Authorization: Bearer <your_device_token>
      Content-Type: application/json
    • Request Body: JSON
      Add key: message (Type: Text) → select Shortcut Input.
  8. Tap Done. Whenever a matching bank SMS arrives, your iPhone securely sends it to your ledger!
