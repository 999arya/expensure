What happens next

  1. The app reads the rows and finds the date, description, amount and reference.
  2. You see a preview. Rows already recorded on this account are skipped.
  3. Confident rows are created as imported transactions. A date-only row on the same day as your confirmed balance waits for review.

No bank connection or password is involved. Only the last four account digits are stored, and the raw CSV is discarded immediately.
