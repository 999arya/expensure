Keep your balance up to date

Entering bank details does not connect your bank or give this website access to your messages. Start with the balance your bank shows now, then import new transactions.

Android instructions
  1. Save your account first, including the last four digits shown in your bank alerts.
  2. In your Messages app, open a transaction SMS, press and hold its text, and copy it.
  3. Open Expensure in your browser, choose Paste message, paste the text, and preview it. Check the amount, debit or credit, and account before saving.
  4. Review the saved transaction and confirm its time and category before it changes your estimated balance.

Optional automatic forwarding requires a separate Android automation app with SMS access and HTTPS POST support. Expensure has no Android SMS reader built in. After saving your account, open Message setup for the connection settings.

iPhone / iOS instructions
  1. Save your account first. You can copy a transaction SMS from Messages and use Paste message, just like on Android.
  2. For future messages, open Expensure's Message setup and generate a device token.
  3. In Apple's Shortcuts → Automation → Message, select your bank sender and a transaction phrase. Create separate rules for debit and credit phrases.
  4. Use Get Contents of URL with the URL, authorization header, and JSON body shown in Message setup. Choose Run Immediately if available, or approve each run.
  5. Check capture events in Expensure, then review each captured transaction before it affects your balance.

Bank support: you can track any bank manually. SMS parsing currently supports Kotak and Bank of Baroda message formats; other formats may be rejected. Use manual transaction entry when a message cannot be parsed.

How the estimate works: your last confirmed balance plus reviewed credits minus reviewed debits after that balance's time. Older transactions are already included; uncertain times need review. Use Confirm balance to reconcile with your bank app.

Only send transaction alerts. Never forward OTPs, PINs, passwords, or unrelated messages. Automatic capture needs internet access; it does not import your old inbox.
